RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

This Residential Rental Agreement (“Agreement”) is made on 01 November 2026

BETWEEN

Mr. Rajesh Kumar Sharma, residing at 12, Green Park Extension, New Delhi – 110016
(hereinafter referred to as the “Landlord”)

AND

Ms. Ananya Verma, residing at 48, Lajpat Nagar-II, New Delhi – 110024
(hereinafter referred to as the “Tenant”)

1. PROPERTY
The Landlord agrees to let to the Tenant the residential premises at:
Flat No. 3B, Third Floor, House No. 22, Street 7, Saket, New Delhi – 110017
(“the Premises”).

2. TERM
The tenancy shall be for a period of 11 (eleven) months commencing from 01 November 2026
and ending on 30 September 2027. Renewal, if any, shall be solely at the absolute discretion
of the Landlord on terms to be decided by the Landlord alone.

3. RENT
The Tenant shall pay monthly rent of ₹28,000 (Rupees Twenty-Eight Thousand only) on or before
the 3rd day of each calendar month in advance. Any delay beyond 3 days shall attract a late fee
of ₹500 per day without upper limit, which the Landlord may deduct from the security deposit
or recover otherwise.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant has paid ₹1,12,000 (equivalent to four months’ rent) as interest-free security deposit.
The Landlord may withhold the entire deposit for any reason, including normal wear and tear,
cleaning, or “loss of rent”, and shall not be obliged to give an itemised list of deductions.
The deposit shall be refunded, if at all, within 90 days after the Tenant vacates.

5. LOCK-IN AND EXIT
The Tenant cannot terminate this Agreement for the first 8 months. If the Tenant vacates earlier,
the Tenant shall pay rent for the remaining lock-in period in full, and the security deposit
shall stand forfeited automatically. The Landlord may terminate this Agreement at any time by
giving 7 days’ notice without assigning any reason.

6. USE AND ENTRY
The Tenant shall not create any nuisance. The Landlord and any person authorised by the Landlord
may enter the Premises at any time, with or without notice, for inspection, repair, showing to
prospective tenants, or any other purpose the Landlord considers appropriate.

7. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS
All maintenance, repairs, society charges, and structural repairs shall be the sole responsibility
of the Tenant. The Landlord shall not be liable for any interruption of water, electricity,
or other services.

8. GUESTS AND SUBLETTING
Overnight guests for more than 2 nights per month are prohibited without prior written permission
of the Landlord. Subletting, sharing, or allowing any other person to occupy the Premises is
strictly prohibited and shall entitle the Landlord to immediate eviction and forfeiture of deposit.

9. LIABILITY
The Tenant shall indemnify the Landlord against all claims, losses, and damages of any kind
arising from use of the Premises, including claims by third parties, even where caused by
the Landlord’s negligence. The Landlord’s total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed ₹1,000.

10. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
All disputes shall be subject to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed exclusively by
the Landlord. Courts in the Landlord’s hometown (Jaipur) shall have exclusive jurisdiction,
regardless of where the Premises are situated.

11. GENERAL
This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding. Any waiver must be in writing. If any
clause is held invalid, the remaining clauses continue in force. The Tenant confirms having
read and understood all terms without independent legal advice.

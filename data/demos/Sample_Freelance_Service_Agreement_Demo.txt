1. SERVICES
The Freelancer shall provide UI/UX design and related digital services as assigned by the
Company from time to time through email or project management tools (“Services”).

2. TERM
This Agreement starts on 15 October 2026 and continues until terminated. The Company may
terminate immediately without notice or payment for convenience. The Freelancer must give
60 days’ written notice to terminate and must complete all pending work without additional fee.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT
Fees shall be as stated in individual work orders. Payment shall be made within 90 days after
the Company accepts delivery. The Company may reject deliverables for any reason and withhold
payment indefinitely until the Freelancer makes changes the Company considers satisfactory.
No interest shall accrue on delayed payments.

4. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
All work product, ideas, drafts, source files, and materials created by the Freelancer in
connection with the Services shall be the sole and exclusive property of the Company from the
moment of creation, whether or not fully paid for. The Freelancer waives all moral rights
and shall sign any further documents the Company requires, without extra payment.

5. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICIT
During the term and for 24 months after termination, the Freelancer shall not provide similar
services to any client or competitor of the Company anywhere in India, and shall not contact
any Company client or employee. Breach shall attract liquidated damages of ₹10,00,000
recoverable without proof of actual loss.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
The Freelancer shall keep confidential all information relating to the Company forever,
including information that is public or independently developed. Breach may be restrained by
injunction without the Company needing to prove damages.

7. STATUS AND BENEFITS
The Freelancer is an independent contractor only, not an employee. The Freelancer is not entitled
to PF, ESI, gratuity, paid leave, or any employment benefit. The Freelancer alone is responsible
for all taxes. The Company may still control working hours, tools, and methods.

8. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY
The Freelancer shall indemnify and hold harmless the Company from all claims arising out of
the Services, including third-party IP claims and the Company’s own negligence. The Company’s
aggregate liability to the Freelancer shall not exceed fees paid in the preceding 30 days.

9. PORTFOLIO
The Freelancer shall not show any work done for the Company in a portfolio, social media, or
case study without prior written consent of the Company, which may be withheld without reason.

10. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Exclusive jurisdiction lies with courts in
Mumbai only. The Freelancer agrees not to raise any objection on grounds of inconvenience.

11. MISCELLANEOUS
If any provision is unenforceable, the rest survives. This Agreement supersedes all prior
discussions. Amendments must be in writing signed by the Company.
